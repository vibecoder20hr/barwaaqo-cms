Introduction

Connecting Visionary Youth to Lead Positive Change

Barwaqo Forum is a youth-led platform dedicated to empowering Somali youth through knowledge, skills development, innovation, and community engagement. It was founded to create a space where young people can connect, grow, and lead positive change within their communities.

The platform serves as a hub for learning, collaboration, creativity, and leadership. It aims to build a strong, visionary generation capable of contributing meaningfully to the social, economic, and developmental progress of the nation.

Vision

"To build a generation of empowered, skilled, and innovative youth capable of leading a prosperous and peaceful society."

Mission

"To provide opportunities, training, and a platform for youth to develop their skills, advance their leadership potential, and serve their communities."

Our Programmatic Scope

What Barwaqo Forum Does

Empowering youth through seven core programmatic areas.

01. Skill Building

Skills Development & Training

Trainings and workshops equipping youth with Leadership & Management, Public Speaking, ICT & Digital Literacy, Entrepreneurship, and Personal Development skills.

02. Connections

Youth Networking & Connections

Creating opportunities to connect, collaborate, and build professional networks via networking events, peer learning sessions, and job/internship access.

03. Action Projects

Collaborative Youth Projects

Community initiatives including peacebuilding, environmental protection, tree-planting drives, public health awareness, and educational support programs.

04. Business Growth

Entrepreneurship & Innovation

Mentorship, startup management training, innovation contests, and connecting young founders with investors to drive job creation and economic empowerment.

05. Solutions Platform

Innovation & Idea Hub

A platform for youth to pitch social solutions, explore research findings, and showcase creative projects that tackle societal challenges.

06. Governance & Leadership

Leadership & Empowerment

Development programs focusing on decision-making, community leadership, responsible citizenship, civic participation, and youth involvement in governance.

07. Social Transformation & Advocacy

Championing Positive Social Change

Barwaqo Forum advocates for youth rights, economic inclusion, and development by directly addressing pressing challenges such as youth unemployment, irregular migration, educational gaps, and community reconciliation.
