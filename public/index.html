Who We Are

About Barwaqo Forum

Barwaqo Forum is a non-profit youth organization established to unite young individuals and equip them with practical skills, fostering innovation, civic responsibility, and community resilience.

Our Vision

To cultivate an empowered, skilled, and visionary generation capable of driving sustainable development, peace, and prosperity across communities.

Our Mission

To provide educational opportunities, leadership training, and collaborative networks that enable youth to unlock their full potential and contribute to nation-building.
